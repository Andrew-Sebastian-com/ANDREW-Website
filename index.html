Who is this ANDREW, you ask?

You're talking about Andrew Sebastian Sudiro—a programmer, animator, and music maker. What should we call him? That's still a mystery. He was born in Jakarta, Indonesia, and he's an entrepreneur who hasn't exactly made it big yet. He keeps getting back up after setbacks and making unpredictable projects. Honestly, I don't know him that well either, so let's see what he's made.

Am I a programmer?

I mostly make random programming projects—sometimes just to see what happens. I sometimes use AI tools, but the ideas, designs, and concepts are mine. If a project isn't your cup of tea, have you tried building something yourself or coming up with a unique idea? Feel free to explore the code, too. I make these projects for fun and to share with others.

Am I an animator?

Not exactly. I'm not great at drawing, and animation isn't easy for me either. Still, I enjoy making animations, especially natural, simple, over-the-top classic styles. Web animations inspired me to create my own. They're not perfect, and I usually work on them alone, so I treat them as experiments rather than finished projects.

Am I a musician?

I don't compose much because I don't consider myself especially musical. But I enjoy parodying or remixing popular songs, especially video-game music. I like J-pop, orchestral music, and sci-fi soundtracks, and I sometimes combine those styles. Like a science experiment, it doesn't always work out.

If I'm not perfect, why do I keep doing this?

I'm not perfect, but I enjoy experimenting. Making things like these keeps me motivated and excited. Some projects are simple or involve a bit of copy-and-paste, but I still enjoy putting them together. I mainly share them on social media, especially YouTube, to document my journey as a creator who likes to experiment.

Will I keep improving my skills?

I'll probably keep improving my skills. I'm not confident enough to claim I'll be the best, and I don't like making promises I might not keep. Follow my journey and see how I grow over time.

What else do I do?

I do other things, too, such as making audio commentary and gameplay videos, usually in my free time or when I'm bored. New content can take a while because I also have school and other projects to work on.
